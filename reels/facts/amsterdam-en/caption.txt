7 things you probably didn't know about Amsterdam 📍

1. The name means "dam in the Amstel" (first written in 1275)
2. The Royal Palace stands on 13,659 wooden poles
3. People here own around 847,000 bikes
4. 100+ km of canals and about 1,500 bridges
5. It's the capital, but the government sits in The Hague
6. The Anne Frank House at Prinsengracht 263
7. The city lies about 2 m below sea level
