6 things you probably didn't know about Amsterdam 📍

1. The name means "dam in the Amstel" (first written in 1275)
2. The Royal Palace stands on 13,659 wooden poles
3. 100+ km of canals and about 1,500 bridges
4. The Anne Frank House at Prinsengracht 263
5. Vondelpark gets around 10 million visits a year
6. An underwater garage for about 7,000 bikes
